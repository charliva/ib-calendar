# Student Calendar

This context covers the student's time, commitments, and self-directed school work in one calendar.

## Language

**Work item**:
A flexible calendar item representing work the student can schedule or complete. Homework, review sessions, and general tasks are all work items.
_Avoid_: Flexible work, inbox item, homework capture

**Homework**:
A work item originating from a school assignment, normally with a due date and subject.
_Avoid_: Homework capture

**Review session**:
A work item for deliberate study or revision, optionally bounded by a target duration.
_Avoid_: Review capture

**Commitment**:
A calendar item with a fixed time, such as a class, event, or appointment. Commitments cannot be resized when they represent a class.
_Avoid_: Fixed task
